## 2. Customer Acquisition Channels for Creative Agencies

### Channel 1: "The Death of the Client Portal" Positioning
* **The Pitch Angle:** *"Every time you ask a high-ticket client to log into a portal, download Slack, or review on an external tool, you lose deal momentum."*
* **The Value Proposition:** Gigxomi lets creative agencies close deals and manage production directly through WhatsApp and Instagram with **zero client onboarding friction**.
* **Target Audience:** Video agencies, podcast production houses, thumbnail agencies, and YouTube editing companies handling 5–50 monthly clients.

### Channel 2: High-Intent Inbound Lead Conversion
* **The Problem:** Agencies running Meta ads or organic reels get DMs on Instagram and WhatsApp inquiries, but conversations get lost, follow-ups are dropped, and quote sending is sluggish.
* **The Gigxomi Solution:**
  * Visual WhatsApp Flow Builder & Instagram Inbound Webhooks capture the lead instantly.
  * Multi-lane workspace allows sales managers to qualify leads, send quotes, and hand off closed deals to editors without ever exposing the client's contact info.

### Channel 3: Anti-Poaching Security for Agency Growth
* **The Problem:** Agency owners are terrified that if an editor talks directly to a client on WhatsApp, the editor will poach the client and cut the agency out.
* **The Gigxomi Solution:** Editors can read the brief and see timestamped feedback, but client direct-messaging is blocked by the backend Capability Resolver.
