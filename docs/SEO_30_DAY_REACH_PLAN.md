# Gigxomi 30-day SEO and Pinterest reach plan

## Target and operating reality

The campaign target is one million combined organic impressions across Google
Search and Pinterest in 30 days. This is an aspirational reach target, not a
guarantee. The current Search Console baseline is 347 impressions across the
first three reporting days, so one million impressions would require an average
of 33,333 impressions per day and a step-change in distribution.

The work must therefore combine search demand, preservation of URLs that already
rank, high-quality publishing, Pinterest distribution, and webinar conversion.
Publishing 200 thin or overlapping pages would increase crawl waste and
cannibalization risk; it is not the campaign strategy.

## One-million-impression operating model

The target is split by channel so the team can see where reach and website
clicks must come from. These are campaign targets, not forecasts.

| Channel | 30-day impression target | Click-rate target | Website-click target |
| --- | ---: | ---: | ---: |
| Google Search | 150,000 | 2.5% | 3,750 |
| Pinterest organic | 750,000 | 1.0% outbound CTR | 7,500 |
| LinkedIn, Instagram, Facebook and partner sharing | 100,000 | 0.8% | 800 |
| **Total** | **1,000,000** | **1.21% blended** | **12,050** |

At a 2.5% visitor-to-webinar conversion rate, 12,050 qualified visits would
produce about 301 registrations. Track the actual funnel by source. Do not
combine Pin clicks with Pinterest outbound clicks; only outbound clicks are
website visits.

### Cumulative pacing checkpoints

| Checkpoint | Reach target | Minimum signal before escalation |
| --- | ---: | ---: |
| End of day 7 | 50,000 | 25,000 |
| End of day 14 | 200,000 | 100,000 |
| End of day 21 | 500,000 | 300,000 |
| End of day 30 | 1,000,000 | 700,000 |

If reach is below the minimum signal, first diagnose indexing, distribution,
query fit, and creative CTR. Reallocate effort toward winning URLs and Pins. If
one million remains a hard requirement, prepare a paid amplification or partner
distribution proposal for separate approval; never spend money automatically
and never respond by bulk-publishing thin articles.

## Daily cadence

- 09:30 IST: publish or schedule one reviewed article.
- 15:30 IST: publish its first 2:3 Pinterest creative after the canonical blog
  URL returns HTTP 200 with matching title and featured image.
- Seven days later: publish a genuinely different Pin creative only if the
  first Pin and article remain accurate; do not repost the same asset.
- Repurpose the same original insight into one native LinkedIn post and one
  short Instagram/Facebook asset. Link to the article only where the platform
  and audience context make that useful.
- Daily: record Google impressions, clicks, CTR, average position, Pinterest
  impressions, saves, outbound clicks, and webinar registrations.
- Weekly: improve the pages already earning impressions before expanding the
  topic count.

## Pinterest implementation

- Use one public business board named `Gigxomi Video Editing Growth`.
- Use vertical 2:3 images, preferably 1000 × 1500 pixels, with large readable
  text, the Gigxomi name, useful visual information, and no unsupported claims.
- Link each Pin to the canonical `https://www.gigxomi.com/blog/...` URL with
  `utm_source=pinterest`, `utm_medium=organic_social`,
  `utm_campaign=seo_growth_2026`, and a slug-specific `utm_content` value.
- Use a unique Pin title, description, and alt text that accurately describe
  the article and visual. Do not stuff keywords or paste the URL into the Pin
  description.
- The sandbox API is for integration testing only. Public Pins require a valid
  production token with board and Pin read/write scopes.
- Pinterest's native scheduler supports standard Pins up to 30 days ahead but
  permits only ten scheduled Pins at once. The daily Codex workflow can instead
  replenish the queue or use the production API after each blog URL is live.

### Click-first creative rules

- Put the reader outcome in the first 40 title characters: `Get Video Editing
  Clients`, `First Editing Client`, or `Cold DM Template`.
- Use a visual promise the article immediately fulfils: checklist, workflow,
  script, calculator, template, or numbered plan. Do not use vague inspiration
  copy or unsupported income claims.
- For each article, test two genuinely different creative concepts seven days
  apart: an outcome-led cover and a practical checklist/framework. Change the
  headline and layout, not merely the background color.
- Scale a Pin when outbound CTR is at least 1.0% after 1,000 impressions or when
  it earns meaningful saves with rising impressions. Rewrite the title/visual
  when outbound CTR is below 0.5% after 1,000 impressions.
- Keep the canonical destination tightly matched to the Pin. The first screen
  of the article should deliver the promised answer before the webinar CTA.

## Google click plan

- Protect URLs already receiving impressions; do not redirect the four current
  leaders until their intent and signals are preserved on the same URL or an
  evidence-backed equivalent.
- For pages at positions 4–15, test concise, page-specific titles and useful
  descriptions. Hold substantive snippet changes for 14 complete days unless
  the page is broken or misleading.
- Target at least 2.5% CTR for pages consistently in positions 4–10. Prioritize
  pages with high impressions and zero clicks over brand-new topics.
- Make the H1, title, opening answer, featured visual, and Pin promise agree.
- Link every supporting article to one primary pillar and link the pillar back
  contextually. Keep important pillars reachable from the blog hub.

## Daily scorecard

Record one row per day with: published URL, indexed status from URL Inspection,
Google impressions/clicks/CTR/position, Pinterest impressions/saves/Pin clicks/
outbound clicks, social reach/link clicks, website sessions, webinar CTA clicks,
registrations, and the next action. Use seven-day rolling rates so a single
volatile day does not trigger unnecessary rewrites.

## Funnel model

At one million reach, a one-percent outbound click rate would produce 10,000
visits. A two-percent webinar-registration rate would then produce 200
registrations. These are planning assumptions, not forecasts. Report the actual
rates by source and improve the weakest stage rather than increasing publishing
volume blindly.
