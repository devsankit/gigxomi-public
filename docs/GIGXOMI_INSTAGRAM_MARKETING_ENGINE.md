## 1. Core Operating Philosophy & True Market Positioning

Gigxomi is **NOT a timeline video review tool like Frame.io** (which plugs into NLE software for frame comments).  
Gigxomi is **the Client Conversion & Communication Operating System**.

### The Big Moat: Zero Client Friction
* **The Client Installs Nothing:** No apps to download, no portals to sign into, no passwords to reset. The client communicates natively on **WhatsApp and Instagram**, where they already spend their day.
* **The Agency Operates with Total Control:** Behind the scenes, the agency gets a professional multi-lane workspace (Customer Lane vs. Internal Lane), preventing client poaching, separating client talk from editor briefs, and streamlining quotes into paid deals.

```
    CLIENT'S WORLD (Zero Friction)                AGENCY'S WORLD (Total Control)
    ┌───────────────────────────────────┐         ┌───────────────────────────────────┐
    │  WhatsApp & Instagram Chats       │         │  Gigxomi Multi-Lane Workspace     │
    │  • 0 apps to install              │ ◄─────► │  • Customer Lane (Client chat)    │
    │  • 0 portals or logins            │         │  • Internal Lane (Editor brief)   │
    │  • Instant response on their SIM  │         │  • Client direct-messaging LOCKED │
    │  • High-trust native messaging    │         │  • Streamlined quote-to-close     │
    └───────────────────────────────────┘         └───────────────────────────────────┘
```

### Feature Status Governance
* **LIVE FEATURES (Sell, Demo & Educate Now):**
  * **Zero-Friction Client Conversion:** Client chats over WhatsApp/Instagram; agency runs the deal pipeline.
  * **Unified Dual-Lane Chat:** Customer WhatsApp/Instagram Lane vs. Internal Team Lane.
  * **Anti-Poaching Permission Resolver:** Read-only client brief access for editors without client number or contact leakage.
  * **Mobile Companion App:** Real-time chats, delivery reviews, and floating bubbles for teams on the go.
  * **Sales CRM Mobile:** SIM calling, timed lead packs, and offline-first call disposition logs.
  * **WhatsApp Visual Flow Builder:** Drag-and-drop lead intake bots with Meta embedded signup.

* **UPCOMING ROADMAP (Tease, Build Hype & Collect Waitlists):**

    * *Angle for Editors:* "Never get ghosted after delivering a video again. 100% upfront escrow locking."

    * *Status:* Strictly positioned as **"Upcoming Feature / Join the Priority Beta"**.

## 4. Visual Aesthetics & Poster Design System

Enforce the **Black-Lime Design Tokens**:
* **Canvas:** Deep OLED Black (`#000000`) and soft matte surface (`#0A0D0B`).
* **Accent Brand Color:** Electric Neon Lime (`#D7FF2F` / `--gx-primary`).
* **Secondary Accents:** Crisp Snow White (`#F5F7FB`) for typography, Slate Gray (`#7E8AA3`) for metadata.
* **Typography:** Clean, high-tech sans-serif (Geist Sans / Satoshi / Inter) with heavy uppercase headlines.
* **Structural Details:** Fine hairline borders (`rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.08)`), glowing lime accents, chamfered corner cuts, and clean grid lines.
* **Strict Avoidance:** No generic corporate pastel blues, purple gradients, or clipart.

## 5. Automated Feature Ingestion Protocol (Self-Learning Loop)

Whenever a new feature, database model, or route is added to the Gigxomi codebase:

1. **Scan the Delta:** Review `PROJECT.md`, `prisma/schema.prisma`, or `src/app/` for newly implemented features or routes.
2. **Classify Status:**
   * Is it production ready? ➔ Create **Launch / Tutorial / Use-Case** campaign emphasizing zero client friction.
   * Is it in-progress or roadmap? ➔ Create **Upcoming Teaser / Problem-Agitate / Waitlist** campaign.
3. **Extract the Benefit Matrix:**
   * How does this help the agency close clients faster on WhatsApp/IG?
   * How does this protect the agency's team and client relationships?
4. **Generate 3 Creative Deliverables:**
   * 1 Feed Poster / Ad Graphic (1:1 or 4:5).
   * 1 Reel Script with a 3-second visual hook.
   * 1 Carousel Outline (5-7 slides) with a Comment-to-DM CTA.
